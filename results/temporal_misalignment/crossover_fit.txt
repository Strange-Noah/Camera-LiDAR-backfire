FINAL v2: mAP(delay) decay fit and backfire crossover point
BEVFusion camera-LiDAR fusion, temporal misalignment T1 (stale camera frames)
Paired common subset n=5712 (clean, k1-k10, LiDAR-only evaluated on identical samples)

PRIMARY MODEL — exponential approach to an asymptote (replaces the v1 linear headline):
  mAP(d) = 0.6409 + 0.0523 * exp(-d / 317 ms)     R2 = 0.9987
  - Decay rate: 1.22 pt/100ms at ~100 ms -> 0.13 pt/100ms at ~900 ms (decelerating,
    roughly halving every ~250 ms). NOT linear (local decay rates below).
  - Crossover with LiDAR-only (0.6496): d* = 568 ms (exp model).
    Measured bracket: k6 (507.4 ms, 0.6515) > LiDAR-only; k7 (585.5 ms, 0.6488) < LiDAR-only
    -> piecewise-linear interpolation gives 562 ms. Adopted headline: **~570 ms (0.55-0.57 s)**.
  - Asymptote 0.6409 < LiDAR-only 0.6496: the curve flattens ~0.9 pt BELOW the LiDAR-only
    floor. A stale camera does not merely become useless — it imposes a permanent penalty
    that persists even at infinite latency. Confirms and quantifies "no convergence".

REJECTED ALTERNATIVES (kept for the record):
  Linear fit (k1-k10):    crossover=660 ms  R2=0.8661  -> OVERESTIMATES the safe budget:
    a straight line through concave data crosses the baseline too late. Superseded.
  Linear fit (k3-k10):    crossover=650 ms  R2=0.9153  -> same bias. Superseded.
  Log fit:                R2=0.9957 but unbounded (no asymptote); crossover undefined.
  Power law:              R2=0.9957, degenerate exponent ~0 -> equivalent to log; rejected.
  Quadratic (v1):         crossover=1170 ms R2=0.9905 -> artifact of forced symmetry;
    contradicted by the monotone flattening. Rejected.
