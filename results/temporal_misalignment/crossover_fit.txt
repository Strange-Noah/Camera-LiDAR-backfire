Notes:
- Safe latency budget: ~0.6-0.7 s. The mAP(delay) curve crosses the LiDAR-only
  baseline at 0.56-0.69 s depending on series/fit; most robust single number: ~0.65 s,
  conservatively 0.6 s. Consistent with measured points (k7 ~0.59 s ties LiDAR-only;
  k8 ~0.68 s falls -0.28 pt below).
- Decay rate in the linear regime: ~0.42 mAP points per 100 ms of camera latency.
- Past the crossover the negative gain DEEPENS monotonically (k9 -0.64 pt, k10 -0.80 pt
  below LiDAR-only); the curve shows no sign of converging back to the LiDAR-only level:
  a stale camera does not merely become useless, it keeps actively degrading fusion.
- mAOE at k10 (0.3210) remains far above LiDAR-only (0.2927): stale camera images keep
  corrupting orientation at ~0.85-0.95 s (mATE 0.2887 vs 0.2823 as well).
- Delays for k1-k8 of the full series are driver-measured; k9/k10 delays are
  camhist timestamp estimates (same method that matches k1-k5 within ~5 ms).
- The n=1198 subset is interim; final numbers will use the ~5,700-token common subset
  once k9/k10 inference completes.
