## 1. Experiment definition (T1: stale image + current alignment)

Per the team spec (Experiment T section), T1 is:

- For each of the 6 camera images, replace with the **real historical frame k steps back** along the `sample_data.prev` chain (k ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, 5});
- **Keep all calibration, poses, LiDAR points and GT of the current sample** (i.e., the Section 16 open question is handled with the "current-alignment" option, as finalized in the spec);
- LiDAR input is unchanged; only the camera timestamps are misaligned.

**Measured latency distribution** (replacement table `sub_table5.json`, n = 35,214 entries per setting):

| Setting | Actual latency (current − historical frame) |
|---|---|
| k=1 | mean 77.3 ms, median 100 ms, range 43–252 ms |
| k=2 | mean 168.4 ms, median 150 ms, range 140–400 ms |
| k=3 | mean 254.4 ms, median 250 ms, range 239–500 ms |
| k=4 | mean 331.8 ms, median 350 ms, range 295–600 ms |
| k=5 | mean 422.5 ms, median 400 ms, range 395–750 ms |

**Eligible set**: among the 6019 samples, scene-first-frames are ineligible by construction; the eligible sets for k1–k4 are identical = **5869**, and for k5 = **5863** (6 scenes have fewer than 6 frames). All comparisons are paired on the common subset of **5863**.

## 5. Conclusions

1. **Monotonic degradation**: mAP/NDS decline steadily with misalignment magnitude (0.6856 → 0.6545). mATE peaks at k3 (0.2898) then falls back toward the LiDAR-only level (0.2831), while mAOE keeps rising — the older the image, the more it behaves like "no image at all", and fusion performance converges toward LiDAR-only.
2. **Not yet backfired, but closing in**: at k=5 (mean 423 ms) the fusion model's mAP advantage over LiDAR-only is down to **+0.64 pt** (NDS only +0.13 pt). Extrapolating the convergence rate of −0.35~0.5 pt/k, the crossover (backfire) is expected around **k≈7±2, i.e. roughly 0.6–0.9 s of misalignment**.
3. Next step: run **k=6, 7, 8 (optionally k=10)** to locate the true backfire threshold; once the crossover is confirmed, we can analyse how the "negative gain" magnitude grows with further misalignment.
