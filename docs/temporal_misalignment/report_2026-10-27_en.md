## 1. Experiment definition (T1: stale image + current alignment)

Per the team spec (Experiment T section), T1 is:

- For each of the 6 camera images, replace with the **real historical frame k steps back** along the `sample_data.prev` chain (k ∈ {1..8});
- **Keep all calibration, poses, LiDAR points and GT of the current sample** (i.e., the Section 16 open question is handled with the "current-alignment" option, as finalized in the spec);
- LiDAR input is unchanged; only the camera timestamps are misaligned.

**Key fact**: one prev-chain step is only ~50–100 ms (the chain walks all camera frames at 12–20 Hz, not just keyframes), so integer k already provides ~100 ms granularity — no extra machinery is needed to localize the backfire point.

## 3. Engineering notes

- ~500 officially mis-packaged nuScenes sweep files (pkl records `samples/LIDAR_TOP/X`, tar ships them under `sweeps/LIDAR_TOP/`); fixed by hardlinking to the pkl-recorded locations, verified MISSING = 0. The cycling inference pipeline additionally applies **dual-placement hardlinks** (both `samples/` and `sweeps/`) for every referenced lidar file, eliminating loader path issues entirely;
- mmdet3d loader path rules: sample-level lidar/img are resolved as `data_root/samples/<CAM>/ + bare filename`, sweeps by the original pkl path;
- During k6-8, most lidar data in `chunk` had been removed by an earlier disk cleanup → built a **blob-cycling pipeline**: per 600-sample batch, targetedly extract that batch's lidar from the 10 tar blobs (3-blob parallel scans) → joint k6/7/8 inference → accumulate → delete the batch lidar; with token→blob memory, 98% early-stop, and automatic skip of unrecoverable tokens (6 skipped);
- k6-8 historical image extraction added 31,584 images (33 files genuinely absent from the official blobs, affecting only 38 k6 samples); k9/10 historical images were already 100% on disk due to chain overlap (only 1 file re-extracted).

## 5. Conclusions

1. **Backfire confirmed (key finding)**: at k=7 (mean 586 ms) fusion mAP 0.6488 is already below LiDAR-only 0.6496 (−0.08 pt, edge of noise); at k=8 (677 ms) the gap widens to −0.26 pt (NDS 0.34 pt lower) — the point where "wrong images become worse than no images" lies at **k ≈ 7, i.e. ~0.55–0.57 s of misalignment**.
2. **The negative gain deepens, and the asymptote sits below LiDAR-only**: k=9 (−0.48 pt) and k=10 (−0.64 pt) keep degrading; the exponential fit's asymptote is 0.6409, ~0.9 pt below LiDAR-only, where the curve flattens permanently — a stale camera does not merely become useless; it imposes a **permanent penalty** and never converges back to the LiDAR-only level.
3. **Decay shape and crossover fit (v2 revision)**: mAP(delay) is a **concave, decelerating decay**, not linear — the local decay rate falls from 1.22 pt/100 ms at ~100 ms to 0.15 pt/100 ms at ~900 ms. An exponential-approach model, mAP = 0.6409 + 0.0523·exp(−d/317 ms), fits with **R²=0.999** and crosses LiDAR-only at **568 ms** (piecewise interpolation of the measured k6–k7 bracket: 562 ms). Headline "safe latency budget": **≈0.55–0.57 s** (the v1 linear-fit 660 ms was biased high by fitting a straight line through concave data; rejected). Crucially, the asymptote 0.6409 sits **below** LiDAR-only 0.6496 (~0.9 pt): a stale camera does not merely become useless — it imposes a **permanent penalty** and never converges back to the LiDAR-only level. Fit details and figure: `results/temporal_misalignment/crossover_fit.{txt,svg,png}`.
4. **Mechanism signals**: mATE peaks at k=3 (0.2894) then falls back toward the LiDAR-only level (0.2829), but mAOE keeps worsening past the crossover (k9 0.3081 / k10 0.3076, ~1.6 pt worse than LiDAR-only) — geometric value is "exhausted" as images go stale, yet they keep actively misleading orientation (mATE at k10 0.2879 shows position starting to be corrupted too).
5. T1 is now complete: k1–k10 inference and paired evaluation all done; backfire location (k ≈ 7, ~0.55–0.57 s), negative-gain depth (k10 −0.64 pt, asymptotic −0.9 pt) and mechanism (persistent mAOE degradation + asymptote below LiDAR-only) are all quantified.
